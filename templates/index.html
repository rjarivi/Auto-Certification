Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Built for people who run events, courses, and workshops — not for enterprise IT teams.

Visual certificate designer

Drag text fields to exactly the right position on your background. Choose fonts, sizes, colors, and alignment per field.

Excel / CSV support

Works with any spreadsheet. Required columns: Name, Email, Course/Event, Date. Download our sample to see the format.
